About

In service of
great ideas.

Cory Anne Roberts is a Los Angeles-based creative producer and art director with the range to take a project from concept to delivery, end to end.

Specialisms span art direction, creative production, casting, photography, retouching, and a keen eye for styling, with deep operational fluency across team building, budget management, call sheets, admin, invoicing, and talent outreach.

As of 2026, Cory holds her first Executive Producer credit, having parlayed a theatrical distribution deal for Michael Leo D'Angelo's psychological thriller The Muse, picked up by Magenta Edge, a new slate under Bob Yari's Magenta Light, with a theatrical release slated for Fall 2026.

  • Based in Los Angeles
  • Far From Home Films
  • 10+ Years Experience

What I Do

Concept through final deliverable, built, directed, and produced.

01

Creative Production

End-to-end production from concept to delivery. Cory builds and leads full creative teams, managing budgets, call sheets, location logistics, crew coordination, talent outreach, and invoicing. Experienced in influencer acquisition for brand partnerships and collaborations, as well as influencer gifting event production. Every project is executed with precision, care, and a global creative sensibility.

02

On-Set Art Direction

On the day, Cory is the eye behind the vision, directing talent through poses and movement, making real-time styling adjustments, and overseeing set design and prop styling to ensure every frame is intentional. The result is imagery that feels both considered and alive.

03

Casting

Talent casting and curation for commercial and music campaigns, finding the faces that carry a brand. Selected reels include Bad Bunny, Von Dutch, and Blacksmith Clothing Co..

04

Photography

Shooting across fashion, editorial, music, film, and brand, with clients ranging from global campaigns to intimate portraits. Every frame is intentional.

05

Executive Production

Now carrying her first EP credit on The Muse, overseeing creative vision, production coordination, and stakeholder relationships, and opening channels for filmmakers across investment, distribution, and production through her film development company, Far From Home Films.

Now in Production · Fall 2026

Executive Producer

The Muse

Psychological Thriller

Directed by Michael Leo D'Angelo. Cory parlayed the theatrical distribution deal that landed The Muse with Magenta Edge, a new slate under Bob Yari's Magenta Light, marking her first Executive Producer credit. Theatrical release: Fall 2026.

Whether you are looking to invest in the film industry, find a distribution channel for your film, or get a strong script produced, Cory has those open channels for filmmakers through her film development company, Far From Home Films.

Released 2023 · Thunderbird Films

Lead Actress · Premiere Producer

Model House

Horror · Thriller

A Thunderbird Films production directed by Derek Pike. Due to no premiere plan in place, Cory and her creative partners built a full-scale Hollywood moment from nothing — black-tie dress code, custom step and repeat, three photographers, a bespoke keepsake popcorn box, and 150 guests hand-picked across influencers, industry power players, and press. The event generated a wave of organic buzz that outlasted the release.

Now streaming on Apple TV and Amazon.
